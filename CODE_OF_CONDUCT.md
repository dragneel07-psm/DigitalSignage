# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully regardless of background, identity, experience,
or ability. Offer specific, constructive feedback and respect others' time.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publishing
private information without permission are not acceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces. Report
concerns privately to manyalp12345@gmail.com. The maintainer may remove content,
warn participants, or restrict participation according to the severity and
repetition of the behavior. Reports will be handled as privately as practicable.
